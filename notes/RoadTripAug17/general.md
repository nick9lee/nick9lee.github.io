# Road Trip: Vancouver → Cannon Beach → Redwoods → Vancouver

**Dates:** August 17–21, 2026 (Mon–Fri)  
**Return:** Back in Vancouver by late afternoon (3–6 PM) on Fri Aug 21  
**Travellers:** 2 people  
**Accommodation note:** 10–15% Booking.com discount available

---

## Route Overview

| Leg | From → To | Distance | Drive Time (approx) |
|-----|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Vancouver, BC → Seattle → Cannon Beach, OR | ~390 mi / 630 km | ~8–9 hrs (with Seattle stop) |
| 2 | Cannon Beach → Florence/Oregon Dunes | ~165 mi / 265 km | ~3.5–4 hrs |
| 3 | Florence → Crescent City, CA (Redwoods) | ~260 mi / 420 km | ~4.5–5 hrs |

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 — Monday, Aug 17: Vancouver → Seattle → Cannon Beach

- **Depart:** Very early morning (~4–5 AM) from Vancouver
- **Border crossing:** Peace Arch (open 24 hrs) — at 5 AM expect minimal wait (5–15 min)
- **Stop (optional): Seattle / Pike Place Market** (~2.5 hrs from Vancouver)
  - Market opens at 7 AM for breakfast spots
  - Grab breakfast + coffee + road trip snacks
  - [Pike Place Market](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Pike+Place+Market,+Seattle,+WA) — 30–60 min stop
  - Quick eats: Pike Place Chowder, Mee Sum Pastry (bao), Lowell's Restaurant, Piroshky Piroshky
  - Snacks to grab: Chukar Cherries, Pike Place Nuts, smoked salmon jerky
  - *Or just skip it and keep driving — it's a tourist trap honestly*
- **Route:** I-5 south from Seattle → Portland area → US-26 west to coast
- **Arrive Cannon Beach:** Early-mid afternoon (~1–2 PM)
- **Afternoon/Evening:** Walk the beach, see Haystack Rock, Ecola State Park viewpoint, dinner in town
- **Note:** Cannon Beach is a quick-hit — beautiful for a sunset walk and dinner, not a full-day spot

**Drive:** ~8–9 hrs total (Van → Seattle: 2.5 hrs, Seattle → Cannon Beach: 4.5 hrs) + stops

### Day 2 — Tuesday, Aug 18: Cannon Beach → Oregon Dunes

- **Morning:** Quick last look at Cannon Beach, grab coffee, depart by ~8–9 AM
- **Route:** US-101 south along the Oregon Coast
- **Possible stops en route:**
  - Tillamook Creamery (~1 hr south) — cheese tasting, ice cream
  - Cape Perpetua / Thor's Well (if tide is right)
- **Arrive Florence/Reedsport area:** Early afternoon
- **Afternoon: Oregon Dunes ATV/UTV adventure** (2–3 hrs)
- **Overnight:** Florence or Reedsport area

**Drive:** ~3.5–4 hrs (Cannon Beach to Florence) + stops

### Day 3 — Wednesday, Aug 19: Oregon Dunes → Redwoods

- **Depart:** Morning (~8–9 AM)
- **Route:** US-101 south through southern Oregon coast into California
- **Possible stops:**
  - Samuel Boardman Scenic Corridor (stunning sea cliffs, short walks)
  - Gold Beach or Brookings for lunch
- **Arrive:** Crescent City / Redwoods area by mid-afternoon
- **Afternoon:** Explore Redwood National and State Parks
  - Stout Memorial Grove (Jedediah Smith) — easy flat walk among giants
  - Howland Hill Road (unpaved scenic drive through old growth)
  - Lady Bird Johnson Grove Trail (1.5 mi loop, elevated grove)
  - Fern Canyon (if time — otherworldly fern-covered canyon walls)

**Drive:** ~4.5–5 hrs (Florence to Crescent City) + stops

### Day 4 — Thursday, Aug 20: Redwoods → Crater Lake → Portland

- **Depart:** Early morning (~6–7 AM)
- **Route:** US-199 north to Grants Pass → Hwy 62 east to Crater Lake → I-5 north to Portland
- **Stop 1: Crater Lake National Park** (~2 hrs from Grants Pass)
  - Rim Village viewpoint + short walks
  - Drive part of 33-mile Rim Drive
  - Budget 2–3 hrs at the lake
- **Stop 2 (optional): Ashland, OR** (on the way, charming town, Lithia Park)
- **Arrive Portland:** Evening (~7–8 PM)
- **Evening:** Dinner in Portland (Pearl District, food carts on Hawthorne, etc.)

**Drive:** ~7–8 hrs total with Crater Lake detour (Crescent City → Grants Pass: 2 hrs, Grants Pass → Crater Lake: 2 hrs, Crater Lake → Portland: 3 hrs)

### Day 5 — Friday, Aug 21: Portland → Columbia River Gorge → Vancouver

- **Morning:** Explore Portland (pick one or two)
  - [Forest Park](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Forest+Park,+Portland,+OR) — 5,200-acre urban forest, Wildwood Trail
  - [Washington Park](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Washington+Park,+Portland,+OR) — Japanese Garden, Rose Garden, Hoyt Arboretum
  - [Powell's Books](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Powell's+City+of+Books,+Portland,+OR) — world's largest independent bookstore
- **Late morning:** Head east on I-84 to Columbia River Gorge (~30 min from downtown)
  - [Multnomah Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Multnomah+Falls,+OR) — 620-ft waterfall, short paved walk to viewpoint (30 min from Portland)
  - [Latourell Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Latourell+Falls,+OR) — closest gorge waterfall to Portland, less crowded
  - [Crown Point / Vista House](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Vista+House,+OR) — panoramic gorge views
- **Depart Portland area:** ~12–1 PM
- **Route:** I-5 north through Washington
- **Border crossing:** Friday afternoon — expect 30–60 min at Peace Arch
- **Arrive Vancouver:** ~5–6 PM

**Drive:** ~5–6 hrs (Portland to Vancouver) + gorge side trip adds ~1 hr

## Tide Pools (along the route)

Best visited **1–2 hours before low tide** — check [Surfline tide chart](https://www.surfline.com/tide-charts/cannon-beach/5842041f4e65fad6a77089b8) for your dates.

### Tide Schedule (Cannon Beach area, Aug 17–19, 2026)

| Date | Low Tides | High Tides | Best Tide Pool Window |
|------|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| **Mon Aug 17** | 9:46 AM (1.7 ft), 10:41 PM (1.5 ft) | 3:51 AM (7.5 ft), 4:09 PM (8.7 ft) | **~8:30–10 AM** or evening after 9:30 PM |
| **Tue Aug 18** | 10:18 AM (2.6 ft), 11:38 PM (1.7 ft) | 4:43 AM (6.6 ft), 4:46 PM (8.5 ft) | ~9–10:30 AM (decent, not great) |
| **Wed Aug 19** | 10:52 AM (3.5 ft) | 5:46 AM (5.8 ft), 5:27 PM (8.2 ft) | Tides aren't ideal this day — focus on Redwoods |

**Verdict:** Your best tide pool window is **Monday morning (Aug 17)** around 8:30–10 AM — but you'll be driving through Washington then. The evening low on Mon (10:41 PM at 1.5 ft) could work for a twilight explore after dinner since sunset is 8:23 PM. By Tue/Wed the tides are higher and less ideal.

**Recommendation:** Hit Haystack Rock or Indian Beach on **Monday evening around 9:30–10 PM** (it'll still be light enough just after sunset, tide drops to 1.5 ft). This is your best shot for good pools during the trip.

| Spot | Where (Day) | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| [Haystack Rock](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Haystack+Rock,+Cannon+Beach,+OR) | Cannon Beach (Day 1) | The classic — walk right up at low tide. Sea stars, anemones, urchins, mussels. Also tufted puffins nesting in summer! |
| [Indian Beach (Ecola State Park)](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Indian+Beach,+Ecola+State+Park,+OR) | Cannon Beach (Day 1) | South end of the beach. Less crowded than Haystack Rock, mussels, small sculpins, sea urchins |
| [Ecola Point](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ecola+Point,+OR) | Cannon Beach (Day 1) | North end of Crescent Beach below the viewpoint — best intertidal area near Cannon Beach |
| [Yaquina Head Outstanding Natural Area](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Yaquina+Head+Outstanding+Natural+Area,+OR) | Newport area (Day 2, if stopping) | Cobble Beach — large flat rocks loaded with pools. $7/vehicle. One of the coast's best |
| [Cape Perpetua tide pools](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cape+Perpetua+Scenic+Area,+OR) | Near Florence (Day 2) | Thor's Well + Captain Cook Trail — rocky shelf with pools and spouting horn at mid-high tide |
| [Harris Beach State Park](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Harris+Beach+State+Park,+OR) | Brookings (Day 3, en route) | Rocky outcrops with tide pools, sea stacks offshore |

**Pro tip:** Negative tides (shown as minus numbers on tide charts) are the absolute best — the lower the tide, the more exposed and the more critters you'll see.

## Inland Return: Nature Stops & Recommendations

### Crater Lake National Park (Day 4 highlight)
- **What:** Oregon's only national park. Deepest lake in the US (1,943 ft). Impossibly blue water filling a volcanic caldera.
- **Time needed:** 2–3 hrs for Rim Village + portion of Rim Drive
- **Entry fee:** $30/vehicle (valid 7 days)
- **Open:** 24/7 in August; Rim Drive fully open in summer
- [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Crater+Lake+National+Park,+OR)
- [NPS Info](https://www.nps.gov/crla/index.htm)

### Columbia River Gorge (Day 5 highlight)
- **What:** Massive river canyon with dozens of waterfalls, dramatic basalt cliffs
- **Top picks for a quick visit:**
  - Multnomah Falls (paved, easy, iconic) — [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Multnomah+Falls,+OR)
  - Latourell Falls (short loop trail, 2.4 mi) — [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Latourell+Falls,+OR)
  - Wahclella Falls (moderate, 2 mi round trip) — [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Wahclella+Falls+Trail,+OR)
- [Google Maps (Gorge)](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Columbia+River+Gorge+National+Scenic+Area)

### Other Inland Options (if you have time)

## Key Notes

- **Border:** Bring passports. Peace Arch is 24 hrs — crossing at 5 AM means near-zero wait. Friday PM northbound expect 30–60 min.
- **Seattle stop:** Pike Place Market opens at 7 AM if you want breakfast, but totally skippable.
- **Gas:** Fill up in Washington (cheaper than BC) and watch fuel levels on rural OR/CA coast.
- **Redwoods tip:** Jedediah Smith and Prairie Creek are the best groves. Fern Canyon requires a $12 parking pass (CA day use).
- **Crater Lake tip:** $30/vehicle entry. Rim Drive is fully open in August. No reservation needed.
- **Coastal driving:** US-101 is scenic but slower (winding, 2-lane sections). Budget extra time.
- **Portland:** Multnomah Falls is 30 min east of downtown. Can combine with a quick gorge stop on the way north.
- **Booking strategy:** Book Cannon Beach early — August is peak season and it fills up fast.
